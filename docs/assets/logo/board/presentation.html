What we heard

The brief

PP Agent is a desktop AI agent for people in finance, sales, operations and HR. It should feel approachable and trustworthy, a calm tool for non-developers that asks before it acts.

approachabletrustworthycalmorganic

Success: recognisable at 32 px, distinct from the usual AI sparkle, works in one colour, and carries over from the current blue identity.

Final design

Growing Leaves

Two layers of leaves grow around one core, getting larger toward the end of the turn: many inputs, one agent.

  • Leaves in two overlapping layers keep the radial feel of the current mark, with a clear direction of growth.
  • The translucent layers echo the product's layered panels, and the single bright core stands for the one agent.
  • A flat one-colour version and a bolder small-size cut keep it readable from 16 px to signage.
