---
title: Connect company software
description: Let PP Agent look things up in your CRM, help desk, and internal tools, with access set per assistant.
---

PP Agent can look things up and work inside company software: your CRM, help desk, document store, or internal tools.

## Who connects it?

Usually, your IT team connects the software once. After that, you just choose an assistant that is allowed to use it.

If you manage the connection:

1. Open the company connections area in **Settings**.
2. Choose the software to connect.
3. Sign in with your company account when asked.
4. Choose which assistants may use it.

Each assistant only sees the software and features it is given. Connecting the finance assistant does not let every assistant use it.

## Check a connection

If an assistant cannot find the data, open the company connections area in **Settings** and check the status. Most problems are an expired sign-in; signing in again fixes it.

## Approvals

Looking at data usually does not ask. Creating, changing, or deleting data asks you first, unless your IT team has allowed it automatically.

## If you need help

Send your IT team the name of the software and the error you see. They have the technical notes for checking the connection.
