---
title: The side panel
description: Open files, a browser, and a command window next to the conversation when you need them.
---

Press **Cmd+J** (**Ctrl+J** on Windows and Linux) to open the panel beside the conversation. It has three parts: **Files**, **Browser**, and **Terminal**.

## Files

- View documents and photos inside the app.
- Attach a file to your next message.
- See files PP Agent just created or changed.

## Browser

- Open a website next to the conversation.
- Keep separate sign-in profiles, such as one for work and one for personal use.
- Ask PP Agent to fill a form or read information on the page; clicking and typing still ask first.

## Terminal

This is a command window for people comfortable with technical tools. If you do not need it, you can ignore it.

## Using other apps on your computer

On Mac and Windows, PP Agent can look at and use other apps if you turn it on in **Settings › This computer**.

- Looking at the screen does not ask.
- Clicking, typing, or opening an app asks first.
- PP Agent, the command window, and password settings are always blocked.
