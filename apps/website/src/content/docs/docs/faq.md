## Is PP Agent free?

The app is free and open source. You or your company pay for the AI service based on usage. There is no PP Agent subscription.

## Does it need the internet?

The interface and your local documents work without a connection. Asking the AI needs a connection to the AI service.

## Do I need technical skills?

No. Your IT team sets it up once. Everyone else picks an assistant and asks questions.

## Which AI can I use?

Whatever your company connects, such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or an internal AI service. Ask IT if you are not sure.

## Does PP Agent send my data somewhere?

Only the content needed for an answer goes to the AI service or the software you connected. Conversations and files stay on your computer. See [Safety and privacy](/docs/security-and-privacy/).

## Does it edit files on its own?

No. Saving or editing a file asks you first. You can set permissions for each tool.

## Does it work on Windows and Linux?

Yes. PP Agent has builds for Mac, Windows, and Linux. Some features, such as using other apps on your computer, are Mac and Windows only.

## How do I share a setup with a colleague?

Ask IT to export the assistant setup and send it over. The person receiving it should review it before use.

## Is there a phone app?

Yes. You can use the phone app, or open PP Agent from a phone on the same Wi-Fi. Your data stays on your computer.

## How do updates work?

PP Agent checks when it starts. You can also use **Settings › General › Updates**.
