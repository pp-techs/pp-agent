title: Connect AI
description: Choose the AI service PP Agent uses, and know who should set it up.
---

PP Agent uses an AI service to answer. You can use the service your company has chosen, or your own account if you are allowed to.

## Who sets this up?

Usually, your IT team connects the AI service once for the whole company. After that, everyone just picks an assistant and starts asking.

If you are doing it yourself:

1. Open **Settings › Models**.
2. Choose your company’s AI service, or sign in to Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, or another provider.
3. Follow the sign-in steps on screen.

## Which AI should I use?

Each assistant can use a different AI. If you are not sure, keep the default your IT team chose.

- A stronger AI is better for analysis, writing reports, and complicated work.
- A faster AI is better for summaries, sorting, and repetitive work.
- You can change the AI for a conversation if your setup allows it.

## Cost

A conversation may show an estimated cost. Your company may have its own agreement with the AI provider; ask IT if you need exact numbers.

## Remove a connection

In **Settings › Models**, you can remove an AI account. Old conversations are still readable, but you need another AI to continue them.
