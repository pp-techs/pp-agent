## Speak instead of typing

The microphone button in the message box lets you talk instead of typing.

- **On your computer:** your voice is handled on the machine. More private, but you need to download a small model.
- **Through an outside service:** the audio is sent to the service you choose.

PP Agent fills the text into the message box. Review it before sending, especially names and numbers.

## Notifications

PP Agent can tell you when an answer is ready, even when the window is in the background.

- Turn this on in **Settings › General**.
- On a Mac, allow notifications when the system asks.

## Tray icon and shortcut

You can keep a PP Agent icon in the menu bar or system tray, and set a shortcut to open the app from anywhere.

## Language

PP Agent includes **English** and **Tiếng Việt**. Choose one in **Settings › General** or follow your computer’s language.

The interface language does not decide the language of answers. Ask in the language you want.
