## Find an old conversation

- **Search** the conversation list in the sidebar.
- **Pin** the ones you return to.
- **Export** a conversation as a text file or web page to share it.
- **Edit and resend** your message to ask in a different way.
- **Branch** a conversation to try a new direction without losing the original.

## Let PP Agent remember

PP Agent can keep notes between conversations. Open **Settings › Memory** to read and edit them.

Good things to remember:

- how your team names documents or reports;
- where important files live;
- the writing or formatting style you prefer.

Do not store passwords, secrets, or facts that change often.

## Keep a conversation manageable

- `/new` starts a fresh conversation.
- `/compact` summarizes the conversation so a long thread stays usable.
- Switching assistants keeps the history but changes the instructions and tools.

## Privacy

Conversations stay on your computer. Only what is needed for an answer is sent to the AI service you choose. See
[Safety and privacy](/docs/security-and-privacy/).
